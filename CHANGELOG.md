## [0.1.0-alpha.1] - 2026-08-19

### Added

- Local stdio JSON-RPC forwarding with bounded startup and request deadlines.
- Deterministic timeout and child-exit errors that preserve only request ids.
- Newline-delimited frame limits and malformed-frame rejection.
- English-first and Simplified Chinese documentation.
