## [0.1.0-alpha.1] - 2026-08-30

### Added

- Loopback-only HTTP capture with bounded headers, bodies, and connection timeouts.
- Durable metadata/body files with delivery-ID deduplication and safe no-ID captures.
- Private Unix file permissions and partial-capture collision detection.
- English-first and Simplified Chinese documentation.
